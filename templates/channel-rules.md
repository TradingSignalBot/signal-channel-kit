## What every signal carries

The market and direction, the entry, the stop and every target, each with its distance in pips,
and the time it was posted ([TIME ZONE]). Every update, a target or the stop, is posted as a reply to
the signal, so each trade reads as one thread.

## The record

The record pinned at the top counts every signal posted here, by these rules:

- **Win:** closed at the last target, or closed at a price in profit.
- **Loss:** closed at the stop, or closed at a price at a loss. A signal that reached a target and
  then its stop is a loss, shown as "TP1, then the stop".
- **Breakeven:** closed at the entry, for example after a posted move of the stop to the entry.
- **Open:** still running, with the furthest target so far.

We never delete a signal, never change its levels after posting, and never add a signal that was
not posted live. If a message is edited or deleted in the channel, the record says so and the signal
still counts. Pips are never added up as a track record.
