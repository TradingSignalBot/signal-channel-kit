## What you should know before acting on a signal

- **Losses happen, often in a row.** Every signal can lose. A run of wins says nothing certain
  about the next signal.
- **Past signals do not predict future ones.** The record pinned in this channel shows every
  signal posted here, losses included. It is a history, not a forecast.
- **Your results will differ from the posted ones.** Your broker's prices, spread, commission,
  swaps and fills are not ours. A signal may reach its target on our prices and not on yours, or
  the other way round. You may see the signal late.
- **Leverage magnifies losses as much as gains.** Size each trade so that its stop costs you an
  amount you can afford to lose.
- **Nothing here is managed for you.** You decide whether to take a trade, its size, and when you
  close it. You are responsible for your trades.

## How the record counts

The pinned record counts every signal posted here: a win when it closed at its last target or in
profit, a loss when it closed at its stop or at a loss (even after reaching a target), breakeven
when it closed at the entry. Levels are never changed after posting, nothing is deleted, and edits
are logged and counted.
